Privacy Policy

App: Postiz FB  |  Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction

"Postiz FB" is a Facebook application used exclusively by its owner to connect the self-hosted, open-source Postiz social media management tool with Facebook. Its sole purpose is to publish and schedule posts to Facebook Pages administered by the app owner, through Facebook's official Graph API.

2. Data We Access

When you connect your Facebook account, the app requests the following permissions via Facebook Login:

  • pages_show_list — retrieve the list of Facebook Pages you manage.
  • pages_manage_posts — publish posts, photos, and videos to your Pages.
  • pages_read_engagement — read engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) on your Pages' content.
  • pages_manage_engagement — manage comments and interactions on your Pages' posts.
  • business_management — access business assets needed to manage your Pages.
  • read_insights — read Page performance insights.

The app accesses only the Pages you explicitly select during setup. It does not access your personal profile timeline, friends list, or private messages.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data obtained through the Facebook API is used solely to publish, schedule, and manage content on your Facebook Pages, and to display basic performance statistics inside your own Postiz dashboard — always at your direction.

4. Data Storage

This is a self-hosted application. All data, including Facebook access tokens and scheduled post content, is stored locally on the server or computer where you installed Postiz (for example, in its local database and environment configuration). Data never leaves infrastructure under your control except for the API calls required to publish content to Facebook itself.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for any purpose. The only external recipient of data is Meta's Facebook platform, via its official API, as necessary to perform the publishing actions you request.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Access tokens are retained until you disconnect the Facebook integration in Postiz or revoke the app's access.
  • You may revoke access at any time via Facebook Settings > Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), which immediately invalidates the app's tokens.
  • You may request deletion of any data associated with this app by contacting us at the email below; we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Security

Access tokens and app secrets are stored server-side only and are never exposed in client-side code. Reasonable technical measures are applied to protect stored data. However, because the application is self-hosted, the overall security of the data also depends on the security of the server or computer where you installed it.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is a business productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
